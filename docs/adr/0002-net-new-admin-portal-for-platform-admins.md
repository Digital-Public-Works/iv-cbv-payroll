# Net-new /admin portal for platform admins, not the agency caseworker portal

The SMS-invitation UI is a net-new `/admin` section operated by Digital Public Works platform admins across all partners. We considered extending the existing agency-scoped `caseworker/` namespace instead, but that would have required resurrecting password authentication for agency staff (the dormant portal has no working sign-in) during a trial where the operators are our own staff. `/admin` ships with no auth and is routable **in local development only**; a follow-up adds Google Workspace OmniAuth (restricted to digitalpublicworks.org) and opens it to non-production environments. Because the operator is cross-agency, the portal carries an agency selector, invitations are attributed to a seeded per-agency system user (resolved from subdomain or selection), and no partner flag gates the portal itself — per-partner gating applies only to communication channels (`sms_communications_enabled`). The agency caseworker portal remains dormant; re-enabling it for real caseworkers is a separate future decision.
